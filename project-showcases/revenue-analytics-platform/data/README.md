# Data and methodology

This portfolio analysis uses simulated marketing and sales data; results describe the supplied dataset, not measured client outcomes.

| File | Grain | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| [Marketing](revenue_analytics_marketing_data.csv) | Month × channel | 108 rows, January 2023–June 2024 |
| [Sales](revenue_analytics_sales_data.csv) | Day | 547 rows, January 2023–June 2024 |

Recorded spend per lead = SUM(Ad_Spend) / SUM(Leads_Generated). Currency, customer identity and acquisition definitions are not supplied. Lead counts are not known to represent unique people. Sales has no channel attribution key. Aggregate each source to month before comparing lead totals; a date-only match cannot establish attribution.

The generation mechanism for the source Cost_Per_Acquisition field is undocumented. The analysis retains it and checks its difference from spend per lead. Before/After labels do not establish causation, and daily revenue is not a subscription MRR measure. [Results and source hashes](../summary.json) are generated by [run_analysis.py](../run_analysis.py).
