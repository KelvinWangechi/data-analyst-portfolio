03 / THE IMPLEMENTATION EVIDENCE
One set of records.
Two independent calculations.
The neutral core uses Salesforce objects, validation rules and six declarative Flows. The KC appendix adds an actual API extraction and SQL reconciliation.
What ran
Authenticated Salesforce REST queries through MCP extracted 23 validated enquiries, 15 related jobs and two rejected intake events. Captured responses were transformed into SQLite and checked against Salesforce’s cost and verification formulas.
The reconciliation passed before this reporting snapshot was produced. This page reads that saved snapshot; it has no live Salesforce connection and performs no writes to the org.

WEIGHTED METRICS / SAME VERIFIED DENOMINATOR
Direct margin =
  SUM(verified revenue − direct costs)
  / SUM(verified revenue)

Revenue per person-hour =
  SUM(verified revenue)
  / SUM(verified person-hours)

Close rate = won / (won + lost)
Missing costs remain NULL. They never become zero-cost profit.
Scope, checks and design decisions
Business case: Prevent handoff mistakes and duplicate jobs, keep quote commitments under human control, and separate known revenue from dependable profit.
Data model: Enquiry → CRM contact + estimator task → approved and accepted quote → Job. Integration Event records preserve rejected intake. Unique external keys make replay safe; completed jobs require a completion date.
Live checks: Fresh intake, contact/task handoff, approval, approval reset after a quote change, acceptance, duplicate prevention, invalid data refusal, completion-date enforcement and cost verification were exercised in the playground. Temporary QA records were removed afterward.
Historical fixtures: 25 enquiry scenarios include 23 valid enquiries and two rejected inputs; 15 jobs include 12 completed and three scheduled. Historical approvals are labelled synthetic seed values. They are not evidence of a live human decision.
Integration limits: This is an actual Salesforce-to-SQL example, not a Workiz integration. Extraction was on demand through the authenticated MCP connection. The standalone Salesforce CLI sync and scheduled refresh are not configured.
For Swans: Explain validation, human approval, handoff and replay decisions using the neutral workflow recording. For KC: Emphasize cost completeness, weighted metrics, reconciliation and the owner’s exception briefing.
