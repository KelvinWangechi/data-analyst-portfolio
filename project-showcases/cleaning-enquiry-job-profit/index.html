03 / THE IMPLEMENTATION EVIDENCE
One set of records.
Two independent calculations.
The core uses Salesforce objects, validation rules and six declarative Flows. Try the live intake above, then inspect the saved API-to-SQL reporting evidence below.
What ran
Authenticated Salesforce REST queries through MCP extracted 23 validated enquiries, 15 related jobs and two rejected intake events. Captured responses were transformed into SQLite and checked against Salesforce’s cost and verification formulas.
The reconciliation passed before this reporting snapshot was produced. The public form uses a restricted HTTPS service to create an enquiry in Salesforce. The existing CRM and Estimator Handoff Flow links the contact and task; a separate REST read-back confirms their relationships. No public endpoint can approve a quote or create a job. The reporting section reads the saved snapshot, rather than adding new enquiries to its historical cohort.

WEIGHTED METRICS / SAME VERIFIED DENOMINATOR
Direct margin =
  SUM(verified revenue − direct costs)
  / SUM(verified revenue)

Revenue per person-hour =
  SUM(verified revenue)
  / SUM(verified person-hours)

Close rate = won / (won + lost)
Missing costs remain NULL. They never become zero-cost profit.
Scope, checks and design decisions
Business case: Prevent handoff mistakes and duplicate jobs, keep quote commitments under human control, and separate known revenue from dependable profit.
Data model: Enquiry → CRM contact + estimator task → approved and accepted quote → Job. Integration Event records preserve rejected intake. Unique external keys make replay safe; completed jobs require a completion date.
Live checks: Fresh intake, contact/task handoff, approval, approval reset after a quote change, acceptance, duplicate prevention, invalid data refusal, completion-date enforcement and cost verification were tested in Salesforce.
Reporting cohort: 25 enquiry scenarios include 23 valid enquiries and two rejected inputs; 15 jobs include 12 completed and three scheduled.
Integration limits: This is an actual Salesforce-to-SQL example, not a Workiz integration. Extraction was on demand through the authenticated MCP connection. Live intake is connected through Salesforce OAuth and REST. Scheduled reporting refresh is not configured; job reporting here remains a dated snapshot.
