# R marketing analytics

My R study includes the exercises and analytical approaches in Chris Chapman and Elea McDonnell Feit's [R for Marketing Research and Analytics, second edition](https://r-marketing.r-forge.r-project.org/).

My next R showcase will connect open customer comments to marketing decisions, combining text analysis with segmentation and behavioural evidence. The design builds on those analytical foundations with an original scenario, data and evaluation. A runnable case will be linked here when it is available.
